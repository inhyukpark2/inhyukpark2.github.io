About

I am an AI research engineer at VUNO Inc., translating modern AI methods into clinically deployable products. I received my M.S. from Ajou University, advised by Prof. Jongbin Ryu, and previously spent two years as an AI Research Engineer at a startup, developing and deploying computer vision models. I absorb the knowledge a new domain demands quickly and apply it rigorously, from model research through deployment.

My research centers on robust learning from imperfect data: settings where data is scarce, labels are noisy, or classes are severely imbalanced. This work spans self-supervised learning, noisy-label cleansing, and class-imbalanced (long-tailed) learning, with further work on multimodal large language models (MLLMs). Although my career has centered on medical AI, medical imaging is among the most constrained domains in machine learning, compounding these data limitations with unusually strict reliability requirements. I believe methods proven under such constraints are not bound to any single domain. If anything, they transfer more reliably to other fields.
