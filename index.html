I received my M.S. from Ajou University, advised by Prof. Jongbin Ryu, and previously spent two years as an AI Research Engineer at a startup, developing and deploying computer vision models. This blend of research and productization grounds my focus on robust, clinically deployable AI systems. My research spans self-supervised learning, noisy-label cleansing, and multimodal large language models (MLLMs) — directions I find compelling because they address fundamental challenges in medical imaging: scarce labels, pervasive label noise, and reasoning across imaging and text. I believe methods proven under medical AI's strict data and reliability constraints tend to generalize naturally to other specialized domains.
