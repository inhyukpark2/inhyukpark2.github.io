About

I am a Medical AI researcher at VUNO Inc., working on translating modern AI methods into clinically deployable products. I received my M.S. from Ajou University, advised by Prof. Jongbin Ryu. Before graduate school, I spent two years as an AI Research Engineer at a startup, where I developed and deployed computer vision models. Leveraging my AI research capabilities alongside this practical experience in productizing AI, I am currently focused on building robust, clinically deployable AI systems.

My current research spans self-supervised learning, noisy-label cleansing, and multimodal large language models (MLLMs) — directions I find compelling because they address fundamental challenges in medical imaging: scarce labels, pervasive label noise, and reasoning across imaging and text. I believe methods proven under the strict data and reliability constraints of medical AI tend to generalize naturally to other specialized domains.

Topics of interest: Self-Supervised Learning, Noisy-Label Learning, MLLMs, Medical Image Analysis
