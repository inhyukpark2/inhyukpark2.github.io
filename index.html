01.About

I focus on building Medical AI systems that are both methodologically grounded and clinically deployable. My current work spans self-supervised learning, noisy-label cleansing, and multimodal large language models (MLLMs) — directions I find particularly compelling because they address fundamental challenges in medical imaging: scarce labels, pervasive label noise, and the need to reason across imaging and textual modalities.

I believe that methods proven under the strict data, reliability, and regulatory constraints of medical AI tend to generalize naturally to other specialized domains. What draws me to this field is exactly this bridge — between rigorous general methodology and real-world clinical impact.
